## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, precision-engineered, yet deeply accessible aesthetic tailored for premium local consumer electronics repair and certified refurbished hardware. It bridges high-tech competence with local neighborhood reliability in Montilla. 

The visual language balances dark-mode technical mastery with pristine, high-contrast light surfaces for crisp transactional clarity. It draws from **Corporate Modernism** and **Tactile Precision**: sharp, high-legibility typography, deliberate dark canvas layers (#0B0F14 base), crisp white contrast cards for high-density diagnostic details, and warm stellar amber/gold accents (#F59E0B) reflecting the brand's signature four-pointed star (✦). 

The emotional response is immediate reassurance: certified technical expertise, transparent turnaround times, certified warranties, and lightning-fast direct engagement (<10s conversion to diagnosis or WhatsApp contact).

## Colors

The palette is engineered around an immersive, deep technical dark mode punctuated by brilliant functional accents:

- **Primary Accent (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24` / `#D97706`)**: The celestial amber gold of the brand star (✦). Applied to core calls-to-action, pricing badges, rating stars, certified warranty seals, and highlight indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (`#408BD1`)**: The cool technical blue derived from legacy diagnostic systems. Used for device category chips, hardware specs, diagnostic status indicators, and secondary navigation cues.
- **Tertiary Utility (`#25D366`)**: Reserved strictly and exclusively for official WhatsApp instant-chat triggers, direct repair quote dispatches, and online technician status badges. Never repurposed for generic success alerts.
- **Neutrals & Dark Surfaces**:
  - `surface-base` (`#0B0F14`): Deep obsidian-slate foundation that eliminates eye strain and sets an authoritative tone.
  - `surface-raised` (`#121820`): Structural wrappers, sidebars, and sticky bottom navigation sheets.
  - `surface-card` (`#1A222D`): High-depth containment for interactive items, diagnostic flows, and spec sheets.
  - `surface-light` (`#FFFFFF`): Intentional, high-contrast white background cards deployed for clear physical reading (e.g., printed diagnostic summaries, paper invoices, warranty certificates, and bright deal cards) paired with deep dark ink (`#0B0F14`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across headlines, body copy, and UI controls. Its contemporary geometric proportions and humanist stroke details deliver immediate legibility, high authority, and a modern European consumer tech posture.

- **Numerics & Pricing**: Always use tight letter spacing (`-0.02em`) with medium to extra-bold weights to convey solid, fixed-price transparency.
- **Conversion Badges & Tags**: Render in `label-sm` or `label-md` with uppercase transformation and slight tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.04em`) to ensure readability on small device components.
- **Headline Scaling**: On mobile breakpoints under 640px, headlines smoothly drop down using dedicated mobile sizes to prevent awkward word wraps on dense diagnostic device names (e.g., "iPhone 15 Pro Max", "iPad Air M2").

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 8-point spatial system guides all intervals. Layout structures depend on screen viewport context:

- **Fluid 12-Column Grid (Desktop >= 1024px)**: Outer margins at `margin-lg` (3.5rem / 56px), inner column gutters at `gutter-md` (1.5rem / 24px). Max container width capped at 1280px to guarantee rapid visual scanning.
- **Adaptive 6-Column Grid (Tablet 640px - 1023px)**: Gutters at `gutter` (1rem / 16px), margins at `margin-md` (2rem / 32px).
- **Single/Dual-Column Dynamic Flow (Mobile < 640px)**: 1rem (16px) margins and gutters. Elements conform to single-thumb touch ergonomics, grouping rapid model selectors into horizontal scrolling segments.
- **Conversion Density (<10s Flow)**: Hero diagnosis modules present primary brand options ("Reparar Móvil", "Comprar Reacondicionado", "WhatsApp Express") within the upper 480px of viewport height on both mobile and desktop.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines stacked tonal layers with sharp geometric boundaries and soft ambient lighting:

- **Level 0 (Base)**: `#0B0F14` flat background. Used for document roots and whole-page canvas.
- **Level 1 (Sub-surface / Structural)**: `#121820` with a 1px solid border in `#243040`. No dropshadow. Used for standard container sections, filter toolbars, and search shells.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards)**: `#1A222D` with a subtle inner border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`). Ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating & Highlights)**: WhatsApp direct contact floating action buttons, repair cart previews, and quote sheets. Features elevated amber or green rim glow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)` for amber brand actions, or `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.35)` for WhatsApp buttons.
- **High-Contrast Reading Slabs**: For physical receipts, certified guarantee certificates, and device specs, invert the layer to pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a sharp black keyline and crisp black shadow: `0 10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a polished, substantial rounded geometry (Level 2 base, expanding to `rounded-lg` and `rounded-xl`). 

- **Primary Buttons & Inputs**: Standardized at `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) to provide large, tactile tap targets optimized for one-handed mobile usage.
- **Cards & Device Containers**: Formed with `rounded-xl` (24px / 1.5rem) to evoke the physical form factor of modern smartphones and tablets.
- **Trust Badges & Micro-Pills**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to frame guarantee badges ("Garantía 1 Año", "Repuesto Original", "Taller en Montilla").
- **The Brand Star Symbol (✦)**: Placed consistently beside repair headlines and guaranteed refurbished stamps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Amber Gold)**: Solid `#F59E0B`, text `#0B0F14`, font weight 700. Min-height 52px on mobile. Hover transitions to `#FBBF24` with a slight forward scale (`1.02`).
- **WhatsApp Direct Action**: Solid `#25D366`, white text (`#FFFFFF`), bold icon prefix, min-height 52px. Floating variant sits 20px off bottom-right viewport with pulsing ping halo.
- **Secondary Ghost**: Background `transparent`, border 1px solid `#334155`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover reveals background `#1A222D`.

### Cards
- **Device & Repair Selection Card**: Background `#1A222D`, border 1px solid `#243040`, padding `1.5rem`, `rounded-xl`. Displays brand logo (Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi), device model name, diagnostic time estimate, and immediate price tag.
- **High-Contrast Reconditioned Product Card**: Clean `#FFFFFF` surface, `#0B0F14` text, featuring device condition badge ("Reacondicionado Grado A+"), battery health, and warranty guarantee seal.

### Chips & Badges
- **Trust Badges**: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, border 1px solid `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`, text `#FBBF24`, pill radius, font size `label-sm`.
- **Diagnostic Category Chips**: Background `#121820`, border 1px solid `#243040`, text `#CBD5E1`. Active state switches to `#408BD1` fill with white text.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Device Search / Serial Input**: Height 52px, background `#121820`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#334155`, placeholder `#64748B`, `rounded-lg`. Active focus state triggers a 2px outer ring in stellar amber (`#F59E0B`).

### Interactive Lists
- **Issue Breakdown Rows (Screen, Battery, Port)**: Row items bounded by `#243040` separators, highlighting component name, turnaround duration (e.g. "30-45 min"), price, and instant "Reparar" trigger.
